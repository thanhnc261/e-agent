Colour decision

Without a cast shadow and with a shallow sidewall, the face colour alone must separate the mark from the ivory canvas. v4's light brand #619581 measured 3.23:1. v5 deepens it one step to #4f8a74 (3.79:1), staying in Soft Lagoon. Dark and high-contrast brand roles already exceed 9:1 and are unchanged. Primary-action accents are unchanged.

The mark is decorative inside a named control; it never replaces the product name.
