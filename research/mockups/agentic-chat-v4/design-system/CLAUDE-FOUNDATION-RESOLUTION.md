## Focused graph review resolution

[Claude's focused review](CLAUDE-GRAPH-REVIEW.md) evaluated the intermediate four-node faceted mark. It did not run tests. The current six-node revision follows that review and has not been re-reviewed by Claude.

- Cube-like reading: replaced the internal three-way junction with one cross-connection; placed a node on every outer vertex. This removes the three-face construction.
- Small-size detail: the supported minimum is now 24px for the full symbol and 32px for its inset app tile. Identity samples and usage contracts agree. Existing header symbols are 24px and launcher symbols are 32px.
- Accessible SVG API: retained decorative SVGs deliberately. Actual launcher and icon controls supply accessible names on the button, as required by the foundation; there is no unnamed standalone symbol control. The reviewer explicitly did not inspect that usage.
- Vertical imbalance: the revised geometry is centered at (16,16), with equal top/bottom extents of 1.7 and 30.3.
