Small by design

Use the bare symbol at 24px and above. Optional app tiles start at 32px. The overlay launcher is a 48px target.

Room to breathe

Keep at least one quarter of the symbol width clear on every side. Preserve the proportions and spaces between nodes. Use either one brand hue or four distinct outer-node colors; the center and connections retain the brand hue. Surface reflections and sidewall shading are decorative. Lockups use the theme's mark and text colors; status belongs beside the identity.

Use a named button around decorative SVGs. The symbol never replaces the accessible product name.
