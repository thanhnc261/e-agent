# Source canon

Every criterion in this skill cites one or more of these sources by `S-nn`. Tiers: T1 is normative or governance material (law, standards, risk frameworks, security baselines); T2 is research-grade human-AI guidelines; T3 is vendor guidance and engineering references; T4 is practitioner libraries and articles. Reading levels: `full` means the whole relevant document was read; `partial` means only the sections named in Notes were read; `summary-only` means only a secondary summary or catalogue entry was available; `unreadable` means the source was blocked, with the reason in Notes. A criterion must cite at least one source read at `full` or `partial`, and a Blocker needs at least one T1 or T2 source.
