## Open items for the owner

- **F-4 and F-7 wording.** The G-0 brief gave one chip sentence ("Uses this task's messages and any systems you have connected; this page sends no extra details.", VI "Dùng các tin nhắn của tác vụ này và mọi hệ thống bạn đã kết nối; …"). It also asked to split the panel into what the message sends and what e-agent uses (question 2). One sentence cannot do both, so its two clauses went to the two parts. The "uses" clause gained the subject "e-agent" that F-4 found missing, and "within your permissions" from the review's proposed shape, which keeps the permissions statement the old hints line carried. Every VI phrase the owner confirmed is kept, but three VI phrases are new and unconfirmed: "Chỉ nội dung tin nhắn của bạn", "e-agent cũng dùng" and "trong phạm vi quyền của bạn". If the owner prefers the single sentence verbatim, it is a two-line change in `i18n.ts` and `routes.test.ts`.
