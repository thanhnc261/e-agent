03 / A restrained visual system

Canvas · #F5F4F0
Surface · #FFFFFF
Ink · #262936
Action · #5555D9
Verified · #26765E
Attention · #95601C

System sans for decisions; a local serif for editorial moments. A 4px spacing base, 10–18px corner radii, quiet shadows, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support. No external font or image requests.

Technology direction

Retain React, React Aria, existing design tokens and the host-owned SSE controller for product integration. This portable HTML/CSS/JavaScript study introduces no runtime dependencies.

AG-UI concerns agent–UI interaction events. A2UI concerns declarative interface descriptions. Neither supplies e-agent’s approval authority; both remain optional future work.

What to evaluate with people

  • Can they explain the action and its limits before approving?
  • Can they find the basis for the supplier choice?
  • Do they distinguish approval from a verified outcome?
  • Can they change appearance or minimize without losing the task?
  • Do they avoid repeating a write after an unknown outcome?
